About Me

My Story

Anyone who knows Mike knows how deeply he loves his daughter. But what many don't see is that becoming a father was one of the hardest transitions of his life.

Mike grew up in a broken home marked by divorce, abandonment, conflict, abuse, and addiction. His biological father walked away from him and his sister, and years later passed away before they ever had the chance to rebuild a relationship. Those experiences left lasting scars.

So when Mike became a father, he wasn't thinking about joy — he was thinking about fear. Fear of repeating the past. Fear of becoming the man he never had.

When his daughter was born, Mike struggled to step into fatherhood. It took over a year for him to fully embrace that role. But in that transition from fear to fatherhood, everything began to change.

Mike discovered a powerful truth: Fear will always be present… but it doesn't have to control you.

Through his faith, he came to understand that Yahweh did not give us a spirit of fear — but of love, power, and a sound mind. He made a decision to show up. To break the cycle. To become someone his daughter could look up to.

And through that decision, he found purpose.
